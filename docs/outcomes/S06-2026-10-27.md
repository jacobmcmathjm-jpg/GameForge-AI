# S06 — bounded animated enemy combat integration

Started after S04 technical validation and S05 integration source on 27 October 2026. **In progress; not full tactical/gameplay acceptance.**

- New non-overwriting isolated scene with three original S04 guards and the shared S03 animation state machine. CharacterController owns collision/root movement; Animator owns the skin; root motion and the animation-lab translation driver are disabled for gameplay actors.
- Bounded four-neighbour 32×32 static-grid search with clearance and physical CharacterController collision. Replan periodically toward the player; stop/fire only with attack range, facing and line of sight. Staggered solid dividers require routes around obstacles.
- Recheck eye-to-target, eye-to-muzzle and muzzle-to-target after animation evaluation. Visible swept projectiles, not an unrelated hitscan, apply damage on arrival.
- Damage interrupts actions; death is terminal until reset, disables the live collider and retains the skinned corpse pose. Session restart clears bullets and restores actors/ammo/path state. Pause freezes action timing, motion and projectiles.
- This is a flat training arena, not arbitrary-level NavMesh generation, stairs/platform navigation, squad tactics, cover selection, crowd avoidance, a wave director or a finished designed level. Multiple guards rely on CharacterController collision; crowd deadlocks and full representative-load testing remain open.

Actual tests/build/render identities will be appended after execution. No visual, rights, play-feel or representative performance acceptance is implied.
