## Quality boundary / next gates

This first presentation recipe is a modest improvement over the empty greybox, not the supplied concept's realism. Detailed character/weapon meshes, convincing material authoring and lighting, properly reviewed/imported Blender props, richer environment composition, sound and representative performance acceptance remain open. Pixel contrast/no-magenta checks are not visual acceptance. Human review is required before treating this as an accepted art direction or expanding the map to a warehouse/extraction level.

No general autonomous realistic-game-generation capability, unrestricted modelling, new navigation capability or full S09/S10/S13 completion is claimed.
