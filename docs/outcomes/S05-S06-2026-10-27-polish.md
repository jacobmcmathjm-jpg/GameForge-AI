# S05–S06 — weapon-handling/contact refinement

User-authorized continuation of the combat-lab polish pass on 27 October 2026. **In progress; not stage completion.** Prior v4 app/game deliveries and original Blender source remain retained.

## Bounded changes

- Time-based exponential aim/recoil response rather than an instant aim offset; reload temporarily lowers the aim blend. Static-cover proximity retracts the weapon/arms without using a world-occlusion-bypassing overlay camera. The muzzle sweep still owns cover collision and damage.
- Original procedural magazine sequence: seated, withdraw, hold, insert, seat. The existing 1.6-second action still owns ammunition/reserve commitment; the inserted visual alone must not grant ammunition. Damage interruption restores the seated visual without a free refill.
- Guard weapon is torso-anchored with final two-hand contacts, not rotated by an unrelated wrist pose. A post-Animator callback solves the weapon/contacts before emitting the evaluated muzzle marker, independent of relative component LateUpdate order.
- Guard projectiles follow the actual evaluated barrel. Range/body-facing/barrel alignment and cover are rechecked at the marker; turning away after the action begins must not produce a magically auto-aimed shot.
- On death the guard releases the live contact solution and retains the weapon on the animated hand; restart restores its original parent/pose. This does not introduce a new polished death clip or ragdoll.
- Kit 0.16.44 recognizes only the exact previous 0.16.43 source set for its protected upgrade. Fresh scene, prefab, reports, preview and build destinations use v5; no old scene/build is replaced.

## Acceptance still open

These remain stylized procedural arms, rigid glove fingers and a modular Unity-authored rifle, not detailed realistic weapon/hand art. Turn-in-place/foot contact, hit/death quality, natural reload interaction, sound synchronization, materials/lighting, full human playthrough and representative rendered performance are not accepted by these changes. No downloads, paid provider, account action, GitHub upload or unattended automation is part of this pass.
