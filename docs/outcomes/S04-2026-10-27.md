## Benchmark and limits

- Original clothed, helmeted industrial guard: layered vest/jacket, cargo pockets, equipment webbing, safety trim, reinforced knees, gloves and boots. Readable opaque visor/respirator rather than a realistic exposed human face.
- Judge at 2–10 metres in Unity and at 0.5–1 metre for the later first-person gloves. Consistent neutral key/fill lighting, front/side/rear plus S03 motion poses. No resemblance to a protected game character is required.
- Maximum 30,000 source vertices / 60,000 triangles, one normalized four-influence Generic skin, five material slots, four 1024-square PBR/normal source maps. Approximate uncompressed source texture storage: 16 MiB RGBA8; runtime import format/mips and vertex splits must be measured separately. No LOD is claimed until implemented/measured.
- This bounded procedural recipe is not a photorealistic character generator. Individual glove fingers are visible geometry, not an articulated finger rig. Human visual and rights decisions stay pending.
