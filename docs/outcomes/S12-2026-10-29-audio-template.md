Runtime variants use the existing bounded pool. Local player shots are non-spatial; enemy shots remain spatial with distance attenuation/filtering. No paid provider, API key, new audio model, asset download or source/rights approval is introduced.
- Standalone audition WAVs are generated from the same shared synthesis code under `outputs/ForgeCore-AudioReview-20261029-v25`. They are saved without automatic playback. Human listening/mix/realism acceptance is false until the user judges them. PCM statistics and successful playback scheduling cannot establish that they sound convincing.

## All-genre architecture and present limits

`GameAudioPlanner` now derives cue requirements from the actual blueprint system graph and exposes them through `GameProductionPlan.AudioPlan` and its outstanding requirements. Racing needs engine/tyre/collision, rhythm needs synchronized music/judgement, strategy needs unit feedback, puzzles need state-change feedback, and narrative needs dialogue presentation. Missing recipes and event adapters are explicit. This is requirements planning, not silently integrated production audio.

The current registry classifies twelve genre families plus a bounded unfamiliar-genre fallback. Four generic prototype campaign builders exist: FPS, platformer, top-down RPG and racing. The richer industrial FPS demo is one specialized profile; this new bank is not automatically integrated into all generic campaigns. Other families need their own gameplay builders. No genre becomes certified through this change, and unsupported genres/hybrids must not silently turn into an FPS. S17 continues one tested genre benchmark at a time; multiplayer, open-world generation and cross-genre hybrids remain separate engineering work.
