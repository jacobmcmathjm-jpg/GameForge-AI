## 3. S11 — menu, settings and checkpoint foundation

Added start/pause/new-game/continue/save/quit menus; sensitivity, inverted mouse Y, hip FOV, master volume, larger HUD text and gameplay sound captions; three graphics presets and 30/60/120 caps. Keyboard/mouse is supported; gamepad/remapping and a resolution selector are not implemented. Normal focus loss pauses the interactive player; diagnostics suppress this so a hidden owned test can run.

Versioned bounded settings/checkpoints use fixed filenames, validation, create-new temporary writes and previous-file backup. Missing/corrupt/wrong-profile/blocked-position saves must not alter the current mission. Checkpoints restore stage, defeated guard mask, vitals, magazine/reserve, position and view heading. They are not exact physics/animation/projectile snapshots. F9 loads; new game explicitly replaces the checkpoint; F5 restarts the current mission without promising to erase its last checkpoint. Music/dialogue and full accessibility acceptance remain separate.

Normal saves are under Unity's per-user persistent-data folder, `ForgeCoreIndustrialDemo-v1`. Editor tests and explicit development probes instead use unique owned test/probe directories. No normal player save is used by the automated diagnostics. A retained `.new` file causes a save failure rather than being silently overwritten; previous state is kept.

## 4. S12 — original local audio foundation

The ForgeCore builder bakes six original deterministic PCM WAVs: shot, impact, footstep, reload, UI and ambient. Recipe `forgecore_original_synthetic_audio_v1`, 24 kHz, mono, 16 bit; source synthesis and hash/sample/byte provenance are retained and validated. No downloads, API calls, paid providers, recordings, attribution claims or licence decisions are needed for this original recipe.

Unity uses a fixed 16-voice spatial effects pool and one quiet ambient loop. Weapon cues follow the actual shot callback; impacts follow projectile collision; reload cues follow the action transition; footsteps follow measured grounded/controller displacement. Master volume, pause/resume and simple captions are supported. Automated runs are muted: PCM/playback scheduling checks do not establish human listening quality. These synthetic effects are not realistic recorded gun audio. Music, voice, advanced ambience/occlusion, refined reload-phase timing and listening/mix acceptance remain open.

## 5. S13 — bounded presets and measurement

Low/medium/high change shadows, distance/resolution, antialiasing, pixel lights and texture mip limits. F6 displays a bounded latest-4096-frame percentile window with whole-sample spike totals/max and allocated memory. Startup/warmup and paused/menu intervals are distinguished; an average FPS alone is not a smoothness claim.

The opt-in development acceptance probe must walk the real controller route, aim/fire actual swept projectiles at active AI guards, perform the objectives, restore a checkpoint and complete extraction at all three presets. It does not use direct guard damage or teleport the player along the route. Additional short warm-idle soak is separate from combat percentile measurement. This is a controlled scripted test of this small demo, not human balance acceptance, an arbitrary-genre generator, a long-duration soak or an indie/AA/full-game hardware certification.
