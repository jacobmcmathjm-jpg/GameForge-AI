They add continuous/appropriate UVs, subtler normal maps, original weathered dielectric paint, board grain, separate braces, exposed fittings, container doors/hinges and small concrete edge radii/markings. Every current bundle retains editable Blender source, exact request/generator, three GLBs, original 1K maps, measurements and actual preview. The old five recipes and original script bytes remain supported unchanged.

These are original bounded procedural recipes authored into ForgeCore during this session, then executed by ForgeCore's production Blender runner. They are not general neural modelling, arbitrary Blender-script execution or evidence that ForgeCore independently authored its own generator. No generation service/API key, paid service or new online art download was used.

The actual Blender and Unity renders were inspected. They are recognisable improvements over the previous coarse sources, **not the reference concept image's realistic quality**. Technical metrics do not certify appearance or rights. The user-approved motion, grounded death support, warm flash, rifle audio and exact six-take recorded hard-floor pack remain unchanged.

## Offline routes and approval boundaries

In the updated app, for example:

1. `Create a refined wooden crate in Blender. Name it my-review-crate.` The fixed route creates/reuses, imports exact immutable source, verifies the installed bridge/kit, compiles and prepares a separate three-LOD/collider Unity review fixture. Old prompts without `refined` keep their old recipe IDs.
2. `Plan refined industrial assets for refined-yard.` Read-only source selection/review proposals and nominal footprint audit. No implicit import, approval or placement.
3. `Review local Blender asset <exact bundle hash> visually.` Independently use `... rights.` for the same source version. `Check local Blender asset <hash>.` reports promotion eligibility; `Promote local Blender asset <hash>.` still requires both real current source reviews. Do not accept a source merely to unblock work.
4. `Review imported industrial asset refined5-wooden-crate-v2 visually.` Or `... rights.` The target-only route identifies the exact local import/source, reuses an exact four-view target preview or proposes separate technical inspection/capture, then opens the verified human review dialog. An optional `revision review2` creates a fresh preview destination; it does not create another asset/catalogue identity. No provider fallback, source promotion, vault store or scene placement follows this decision.

Source and target review are different decisions. The new source/target preview dialogs start unchecked with acceptance disabled and not bound to the default Enter action. The inbox can reject an art review but cannot accept it outside its verified preview dialog. Missing/stale files, versions, hashes, dimensions, imported provenance or image decode fail closed. The executor still rechecks the exact current files before recording a real user decision. Historical/prior-session art inbox items remain reject-only; reissue the exact review prompt when needed.

The currently configured installed Blender 5.2 and Unity 6000.5.5f1 are needed for creation/target work. The delivered app uses the verified offline configuration, not the live configuration or a model/key backup. These fixed routes work without an inference/provider fallback; they do not make arbitrary genre requests work offline.
