## Current limits

These are procedural prototypes and bounded recipes. The platformer uses a capsule; the top-down arena uses simple procedural enemies; the racer is a placeholder vehicle/checkpoint controller; the map is sparse; the animation lab is diagnostic. Source characters are not accepted detailed realistic art. Basic HUD/menu/pause/restart and synthesized feedback are included where implemented, but music/voice production, arbitrary object/genre generation, designed commercial levels, representative smooth performance and reliable unattended prompt-to-finished-game delivery remain incomplete. Neither a still, headless startup nor deterministic objective test certifies those qualities.

Next work remains the staged roadmap, particularly bounded S08 industrial props and separate S09 reviewed target-integration gates. This showcase does not close S01–S07 or implement S08.
